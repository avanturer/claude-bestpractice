## Decision
**A turn ends on a pause, without what a finish owes, when the paused card's blocker quotes the
founder** — a message of theirs in the transcript holds it, whatever its case, spacing or outer
quotes — **or when the Stop gate has refused this session twice in a row before it.** Any other
pause is judged as a finish, and the refusal says why and names the pause with their words.
The card is on the board either way, so it is never asked for. Decision 0033 holds otherwise.

## Why
> изучи все еще раз точно пойми полностью и составь список что по твоему взвешенному мнению
> реально даст пользу и начнем все по порядку в одной сессии делать

Decision 0033 took a pause as a legitimate end because it carries the founder's word
(«словом пользователя»), and nothing checked that it did: a blocker of twelve characters, any
twelve, ended a turn over a red suite with no run and nothing on record. Its own cost line said
so. DeepSeek Harness's goals refuse `blocked` until three consecutive rounds have passed —
"difficulty, uncertainty, or useful remaining work is not blocked" — and let a person stop a
goal at once.

## Rejected
- **Three refusals, as DeepSeek Harness has it.** The fourth block is this gate's ceiling.
- **The founder's word only.** An external blocker — a key, somebody else's merge — is real,
  and two refusals over it are enough to show it held.
- **Matching the blocker loosely.** A paraphrase is the session's words, which is the point.

## Cost accepted
A session stopped by something outside the repository is refused once more before its pause is
taken, unless the founder's words say so.
